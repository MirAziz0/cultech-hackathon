# Textures
All from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), CC0 (public domain). 1k diffuse (_diff) and OpenGL normal (_nor) maps; the newer ones are resized for phones (diffuse 1024 px, normal 512 px).

- Aghdam: painted_plaster_wall, marble_01, marble_tiles, patterned_paving_02, leafy_grass, asphalt_02, grey_plaster_02
- Shirvanshahs: sandstone_blocks_08 (Absheron limestone stand-in), rock_tile_floor
- Maiden Tower: castle_wall_slates, old_stone_wall, rock_tile_floor, dry_ground_01
- Erivan Fortress: red_brick_03, brick_floor, painted_plaster_wall, rock_face (Hrazdan gorge), leafy_grass, dry_ground_01, rock_tile_floor
- Kish church: kish_wall (Poly Haven rustic_stone_wall, desaturated ×0.8 and lightened to the average colour of the church walls in the Commons photos), clay_roof_tiles_02 (the orange tiles of the roofs and the drum cone), grey_stone_path (the flagstone yard), painted_plaster_wall (vaults), rock_tile_floor, old_stone_wall (river-stone walls), leafy_grass, dry_ground_01
